# AquaKart asset-rights register

Status: **verification required before public commercial launch**

This repository contains product/brand imagery under `apps/mobile/assets/images/`. The codebase does not establish the copyright licence or permission for every image.

## Assets requiring provenance verification

- `aquacia_*.png`
- `aquafina_*.png`
- `bisleri_*.png`
- `kinley_*.png`
- `bottle_1l.*`
- `cool_jar.jpg`
- `jar_20l.*`
- `water_splash_bg.jpg`
- `app_icon.jpg`
- `logo*.png`

For each asset, record one of:
1. created by AquaKart and owned by AquaKart;
2. created under a licence that permits this commercial use;
3. supplied by the relevant brand with documented permission;
4. replaced with an original/licensed asset.

Do not rely on an image being publicly downloadable as evidence that it is licensed for commercial use.

## Brand/trademark caution

Brand names and packaging such as Bisleri, Aquafina, Kinley and Aquacia are third-party marks. Showing a genuine product for identification can be different from implying sponsorship, endorsement, partnership or ownership. Keep product/brand descriptions factual and avoid wording that suggests affiliation unless documented.

## Release gate

Before public launch, keep the provenance/permission record with the asset or in the company's internal records. If provenance cannot be established, replace the asset.
