# 22-second Storyboard & Composition

**0.0–2.5s**
AquaKart logo / water-drop visual.
Headline:
“Need water. Right now.”

**2.5–6.0s**
Real customer UI:
Search → water product/supplier → select.
Caption:
“Find water nearby.”

**6.0–10.0s**
Real checkout UI.
Trigger the actual dispatch/search state.
Caption:
“AquaKart finds an available vehicle.”

**10.0–14.0s**
Transition into assigned order / order tracking.
Caption:
“Order assigned. Delivery in motion.”

**14.0–18.0s**
Hard cut to supplier routes/fleet UI.
Show:
* today’s vehicles
* route status
* delivery progress
* opportunistic order offer
Use subtle camera movement and UI emphasis.

**18.0–22.0s**
Three-beat system reveal:
CUSTOMER → SUPPLIER → DELIVERY

Final lockup:
“A local water supply network, in one app.”
“AquaKart 2.0”
